About DES

The Data Encryption Standard (DES) is a symmetric-key block cipher that processes 64-bit blocks using a 56-bit effective key and 16 Feistel rounds.

DES is historically important in the study of cryptography, but its 56-bit effective key is no longer considered secure against modern brute-force attacks.
